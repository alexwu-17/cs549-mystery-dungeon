Controls

Move: WASD or arrow keys
Attack: Space

Pressing Attack always advances the turn, even if no enemy is hit. This lets you stay in place while enemies move.

Rooms light up completely when you enter them. If a corridor tile is immediately adjacent to the player while they are in a room, that corridor tile is also lit. In corridors, only the immediately surrounding tiles are clearly lit, and that light does not spill into neighboring rooms.

Floors 1–4 are procedural. Floor 5 is the final boss encounter.
