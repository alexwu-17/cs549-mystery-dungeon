The logical map remains 42×32. `DungeonRenderer.cave_scenery_border_width` defaults to 6; setting it to 0 restores the previous filler footprint and removes the distant apron. ## Geometry and visibility

`CaveTerrainBackdrop.plan()` can grow a **visual** rectangle independently of `DungeonData.map_bounds`. The distance/clearance calculation continues through that rectangle, using existing coordinate-hashed full/half rock heights and actual structural protection. Interior records are unchanged. Existing outside-boundary filler is respected rather than duplicated. Border records are marked `external`; they have no discovery state and never illuminate because of logical fog discovery. Their global dim/other-elevation palette matches the surrounding background.

Detailed scenery continues six cells beyond each logical edge. Six alone is insufficient when looking past an elevated edge: the narrow perspective can see substantially farther down to ground. `CaveSceneryApron` adds four distant plane strips beyond the detailed scenery, using one shared material and eight triangles. It computes a conservative guard from the actual camera corner rays, maximum walkable surface height, and ground base, plus two cells of safety. The default camera yields an 18-cell total visual margin. This keeps the **outermost** scenery boundary outside normal camera views without extending detailed rock instances across that whole margin. The strips do not cover the playable interior or fill bridge air space.

The apron is static. Its four strips rebuild only during setup or viewport resize. There are no per-frame terrain scans, moving backgrounds, atmospheric shaders, alpha fades, new collision, or new shadow casters. Its simple distant surface is intentionally quiet; continuous geometry/material experiments are later stages.

Ordinary fog refreshes visit the existing interior record indices only. External colors change only when the global elevation palette changes, with one corresponding apron material update. No additional border cells are discovered or added to the minimap. Foundations, terraces, shallow bridge supports, ramps and normal half-height walls retain their original geometry.

## Architecture recommendation for Stage 2 review

Start with an optional **ground-only chunk ArrayMesh** driven by `CaveTerrainBackdrop.records`, retaining the current MultiMesh mode for A/B. Keep terrain heights/clearance decisions in the existing planner. Do not merge foundations, ramps, lower walls or bridge supports into that mesh.

- Merge adjacent coplanar tops only when height and fog/tint treatment are compatible. Coordinate tint differences constrain merging; a later world-space stone material can replace tile-based tint only after visual review.
- Omit shared side faces when a neighboring filled ground volume covers them. Preserve exposed sides and the legitimate air beneath shallow supports. Do not infer solid causeways from upper walkable cells.
- Chunk meshes can use indexed ArrayMesh or SurfaceTool construction at floor setup. Fog color changes should update color attributes or a small shader palette on existing chunks when logical fog changes; rebuilding geometry or rescanning scene nodes every frame would undo the current performance work.
- Existing `fog_visuals` walls/foundations/terraces fade or hide individually. Merging them across ownership/occlusion boundaries requires a separate design, so exclude them from the first prototype.
- For Stage 3, test a shared world-space triplanar stone material on the prototype ground and then compatible cliff materials. Reuse deterministic textures, keep normal/roughness detail subtle, preserve fog/shadow/opacity behavior, and retain the three recognizable foundation layers. Current source assets mostly use modeled detail and colors, so texture mapping alone will not remove cubic geometry.
- For Stage 4, first weather exposed decorative cliff faces with anchored joins. Any bevel/displacement must stay inside decorative footprints and away from ramps/walkable clearances. Preserve full required foundation coverage and bridge depth. Avoid smoothing topology/elevation boundaries or silently relaxing geometry validation.

Stages 2–4 were proposals in the Stage-1 checkpoint. The subsequent reversible implementation and matched checks are documented in [continuous cave terrain](continuous_cave_terrain.md).
